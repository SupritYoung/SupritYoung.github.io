About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Computer Science, Wuhan University, advised by Academician Sheng Liu and Prof. Miao Li. My research lies at the intersection of Embodied AI and Large Language Models, with a focus on Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and Whole-Body Action Models (WAM).

In particular, I am deeply interested in self-evolving in-context learning for embodied AI — enabling robots to continuously adapt and improve through interaction experience without explicit retraining. This thread runs through my work on robot manipulation, dexterous grasping, and human-robot interaction.

Beyond academia, I previously served as an Algorithm Architect at Zhipu AI (智谱AI) and an Algorithm Engineer at Huawei Automotive BU (华为车BU). I am also a drafting member of the Chinese National Standard for Humanoid Robot Datasets, and an expert consultant for the Hubei Humanoid Robot Center.

As the project lead of "Tianwen Robot: Pioneer of Service-oriented Embodied Foundation Models" (天问机器人), I led the team to win the Gold Award in the Industry Track at the China International College Students' Innovation Competition (2025) National Finals.
